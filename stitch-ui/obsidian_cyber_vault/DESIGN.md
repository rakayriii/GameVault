## Brand & Style

The design system sets an authoritative, high-tier standard for digital gaming assets, account exchanges, and escrow-backed transactions. Anchored by the vision "Temukan akun game impianmu," it balances hardcore gamer prestige with institutional transaction trust. The emotional goal is immediate confidence: eliminating fear of fraud while celebrating the thrill of elite tier unlocks, exclusive skins, and competitive milestones.

The aesthetic philosophy fuses **Hyper-Refined Dark Glass** with **Atmospheric Cyber Lighting**:
- **Obsidian Foundations:** Pure depth without harsh pure-black dead space. Layers of deep void obsidian and deep midnight navy create layered spatial context.
- **Controlled Luminescence:** Neon accents (Electric Violet and Cyber Cyan) act as deliberate focal spotlights, status monitors, and actionable triggers rather than chaotic visual noise.
- **Institutional Assurance:** Vibrant emerald trust indicators anchor escrow flows, seller certifications, and instant delivery guarantees.
- **Precision Metrics:** High-contrast, tabular data presentation for account stats, KD ratios, inventory valuations, and skin tiers.

## Colors

The palette employs deep obsidian bases, high-energy synthetic accents, and cryptographic security tones.

### Surface System
- `canvas-default`: `#090B10` — The absolute foundation; deep atmospheric obsidian void.
- `canvas-subtle`: `#0E121E` — Primary application background and secondary surface container.
- `surface-card`: `#151B2B` — Component card surfaces, modals, and container shells.
- `surface-elevated`: `#1E2638` — Hover states, dropdown menus, table headers, and inner floating wells.
- `surface-glass`: `rgba(21, 27, 43, 0.72)` — Frosted backdrop surface with blurred underlying game art.

### Accents & Identity
- `primary`: `#8B5CF6` (Electric Violet) — Primary action state, interactive focus, epic-tier designations.
- `primary-hover`: `#7C3AED` — Compressed/hover state for primary interactive elements.
- `primary-glow`: `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.35)` — Ambient halo around hero elements and elite tier unlocks.
- `secondary`: `#06B6D4` (Cyber Cyan) — Feature counters, tech highlights, active filters, and live bid tickers.
- `secondary-hover`: `#0891B2` — Secondary action focus and hover transitions.

### Trust & Semantics
- `status-success`: `#10B981` (Emerald Escrow) — Verified seller marks, funds held safely, automated instant delivery.
- `status-warning`: `#F59E0B` (Mythic Amber) — Mythical ranks, immortal skins, limited flash deals, low stock alerts.
- `status-danger`: `#EF4444` (Crimson Pulse) — Disputed trades, charge warnings, account binding locks, expired listings.

### Typography & Structure Tints
- `text-primary`: `#FFFFFF` — Pure white for headline punch and primary labels.
- `text-secondary`: `#CBD5E1` — High-clarity slate for account spec details and active navigation.
- `text-muted`: `#94A3B8` — Metadata, timestamps, inactive icons, and helper descriptions.
- `border-subtle`: `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.12)` — Structural boundaries that separate information cleanly.
- `border-accent`: `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.4)` — Active focus rings and premium featured asset borders.

## Typography

The type system balances technical gamer hardware cues with frictionless legibility.
- **Sora (Headlines):** High-impact geometric cut with modern structural wide-apertures. Provides the authoritative, cutting-edge gaming tone across banners, balance sheets, and modal hero titles.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & UI):** Engineered for ultra-clean readability in dark-mode environments. Wide open counters prevent dark-background text clumping and optical vibration.
- **JetBrains Mono (Stats, Pricing, Badges):** Provides unambiguous clarity for critical transaction figures, server codes, account level IDs, and live escrow transaction hashes.

All alphanumeric currency displays (IDR, USD, Vault Tokens) must be styled in `label-mono` or `label-stat` to maintain columnar alignment during scrolling.

## Layout & Spacing

The architecture operates on an 8pt rhythm using a responsive 12-column dynamic grid. 

### Breakpoints & Canvas Bounds
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns, max-width `1340px` centered, 24px gutters, 32px outer canvas margins. Listing card grids scale from 3 to 4 cards per row.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, 24px margins. Filter sidebars collapse into a floating bottom sheet. Listing cards scale to 2-3 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px page margins. Full-bleed cards with 12px internal gutters and horizontal pill-carousel scrolling for game category selections.

Vertical page section offsets strictly require `space-xl` (40px) or double-step multiples (`5rem`) to maintain breathable spacing amidst visually intense game banner art and badge saturation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical stacking, luminescent back-glows, and dark glass translucency rather than muddy black drop shadows.

- **Layer 0 (Void):** `#090B10` baseline canvas.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Modular Panes):** `#151B2B` with a sharp `1px` border of `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 2 (Hover & Interactive Overlays):** `#1E2638` with border transition to `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.4)` and a localized neon bloom: `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -10px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Escrow Checkouts & Sticky Dockbars):** Translucent backdrop blur `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` on `rgba(21, 27, 43, 0.85)` framed by a continuous `1px` stroke of `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`.
- **Mythic Accent Highlights:** Tier-S cards feature an ambient radial glow positioned behind the top right header: `radial-gradient(circle at top right, rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12), transparent 70%)`.

## Shapes

The design uses a clean, hybrid-rounded aesthetic (`roundedness: 2` / 8px base) that reconciles structural utility with sleek gamer interfaces.

- **Primary Cards & Containers:** `12px` to `16px` border-radius (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl`).
- **Interactive Inputs, Form Controls & Normal Buttons:** `8px` (`rounded-lg`) to balance tap area with crisp boundaries.
- **Gaming Badges, Trust Chips & Filter Pills:** `9999px` (Full Pill) for instant recognition across inventory grids.
- **Avatar Rank Frames:** Concentric hexagonal masks or standard circular avatars paired with rank-colored border rings.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Cyber Action:** Background gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #8B5CF6, #7C3AED)`, white text, `8px` radius, font weight 600. Subtle violet edge ring `0 0 0 1px rgba(167, 139, 250, 0.3)`. Hover triggers a bright cyan drop shadow bloom `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.5)`.
- **Escrow / Instant Buy:** Vibrant emerald `#10B981` background, `#090B10` high-contrast bold text, accompanied by an inline locked shield SVG icon.
- **Secondary Ghost:** Surface `#1E2638` with `1px` stroke of `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.15)`. Text `#CBD5E1`. On hover, border shifts to `#06B6D4` with text `#FFFFFF`.

### Listing & Account Cards
- Built using `#151B2B` background with an integrated 16:9 game hero banner.
- Image banner includes a subtle dark bottom fade `linear-gradient(to bottom, transparent 40%, #151B2B 100%)`.
- Top overlays house the "Instant Delivery" pill (emerald glass) on the left and the "Game Rank" pill on the right.
- Account metrics row uses `JetBrains Mono` with muted labels and white stat numbers (e.g., skins count, win rate, account level).
- Price block anchors the bottom-left with a large display price, accompanied by a clean purple "Detail" or "Beli" action button.

### Badge Chips & Tier Indicators
- **Escrow Verified:** Deep green-tinted background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#10B981`, `1px` border `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`. Includes an SVG check/shield icon.
- **Rank Tier (Mythic / Immortal):** Warm amber `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`, text `#F59E0B`, `1px` border `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4)`.
- **Server / Region Chip:** Neutral-subtle surface `#1E2638`, text `#94A3B8`, border `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.1)`.

### Form Controls & Filter Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Background `#0E121E`, border `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.14)`. Placeholder text `#94A3B8`. On focus, transitions cleanly to border `#8B5CF6` with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15)` ring.
- **Dual Slider (Price Range):** Track is `#1E2638`, active range filled with `#06B6D4`, drag handles styled as pure white `#FFFFFF` solid circles with cyan drop shadows.
- **Checkboxes & Switches:** Checkbox checked state uses a violet gradient fill with a crisp white geometric checkmark. Inactive state is an unfilled hollow box in `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`.

### Escrow Step-by-Step Tracker
- A linear progress component visualizing the secure transaction pipeline: *Pembayaran Diverifikasi -> Penjual Menyerahkan Akun -> Pemeriksaan Pembeli -> Dana Diteruskan*.
- Completed steps glow in `#10B981`. The active step pulses with an electric violet beacon ring `0 0 0 4px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)`.
